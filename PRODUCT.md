# PRODUCT

## What the site sells

OUDYN WATCHS is a pre-owned luxury watch dealer in Milano. It buys, authenticates, services where needed, photographs and resells one example at a time. The catalogue is deliberately short: currently nine watches and two straps, plus an archive of references that have left the floor.

The site does not pretend to be a manufacturer. Its product is evidence: the actual watch, its year, condition, set contents, authentication report and the terms that come with the sale.

## Who it is for

People who want a specific modern or vintage reference without the theatre of a boutique: first-time buyers who need the process explained, collectors who want the case and paperwork described plainly, and owners who want to sell or trade a watch without listing it themselves.

## What the storefront must do

1. Show the actual watch. Condition, year and set contents belong on the card and the product page, not buried in a footer.
2. Explain the dealer process before asking for money: authentication, service, photographs, warranty, returns.
3. Keep a short, credible catalogue. A sold reference goes to the archive rather than disappearing.
4. Offer two clear paths: buy a watch, or sell/trade one.
5. Let buyers hear from previous owners through long-form stories, one page per story.
6. Collect an order request and contact enquiry without pretending card payment or authentication exists yet.

## Catalogue model

A published product has a reference (`ODN-05-GMT`), a collection, a category (watch or accessory), a year, a condition, a set label (`FULL SET`, `BOX & PAPERS`, `WATCH ONLY`), an edition label (`{year} · {setLabel}`), a spec summary, photo URLs, grouped specifications, add-ons and an included list. Watch entries carry a `CONDITION` spec group; accessories are new stock.

A buyer story has a customer, location, watch reference, quote, excerpt, body, images, purchase date and a verified flag. Stories are content, not reviews with star ratings.

## Page inventory

- **Home** — Rolex atmosphere hero, dealer statement, studio card, latest acquisitions, shop by family, available-now switcher, OUDYN standard.
- **Listing** — dark photo hero, client-side search/filter/sort, case lineup, four-column photo grid, Show more, service band.
- **Product** — 73/27 photo column and sticky buy rail, condition/year/set, accessory selector, spec table, stat rail, editorial bands, feature tabs, FAQ, press, related references, mobile sticky buy bar.
- **Buyer stories** — list and one page per owner, with the watch that was bought.
- **About** — dealer position, numbers, studio photography, 2016–2026 timeline, service rows, buying code.
- **Sell** — process, what OUDYN buys, enquiry form.
- **Contact** — address, hours, store photography, form.
- **Authentication / Shipping / FAQ / Privacy / Terms** — the policies a buyer needs before committing.
- **Collections / Archive / Wishlist / Account / Checkout** — browsing, saved references, order request.

## Boundaries

- No payment provider, customer accounts, inventory reservation or transactional email.
- Photography is placeholder stock until the studio shoot; the watches pictured are not the watches described.
- Buyer stories are written examples until consented customer material replaces them.
- JSON files are a prototype store; production needs a database and authenticated admin.
